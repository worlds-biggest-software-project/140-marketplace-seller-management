## Legal & IP Summary

Amazon SP-API, eBay APIs, and TikTok Shop APIs are open standards published by respective platforms; no IP encumbrances. GS1 GTIN codes are industry-standard identifiers maintained by GS1 organization. EDI standards (AS2/SFTP) are established industry protocols. OpenAPI 3.0 is an open standard. No software patents identified as encumbering marketplace management platforms.
